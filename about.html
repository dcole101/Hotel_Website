About Us

Who Are the People?

Electric Spuds is an independent animation collective founded by 14 Sheridan College Computer Animation alumni. Bringing together artists from Canada, the United States, and China, we're a remote team collaborating to create original animated stories and continue growing as artists.

Our Process

We approach our projects with an industry-inspired production pipeline, organizing our team into dedicated departments and roles.

Working with Industry and Indie 3D tools, we enjoy experimenting with different software and workflows to find the best approach for every project.

Why We Create

Built on volunteer contributions and a shared passion for animation, Electric Spuds is an opportunity for young professionals to collaborate beyond the classroom, challenge themselves creatively, and bring original ideas to life.
