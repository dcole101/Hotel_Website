⚡︎ ⚡︎ about us_

We're Electric Spuds, an animation collective bringing together 14 emerging artists from across Canada, the United States, and China.

Built on a shared love of animation, storytelling, and all things creative, we're a full production team working together to bring original ideas to life. From colourful characters to expressive worlds and stories packed with personality, we believe animation should be fun to make and even more fun to watch.

⚡︎ ⚡︎ Making Animation _

We create short-form animated stories with a focus on stylized visuals, expressive characters, comedy, and memorable storytelling.

Inspired by the animated films, anime, and cartoons we love, our work brings together aesthetics, familiar tropes, and the unexpected.

⚡︎ ⚡︎ How? _

We do the full industry-level pipeline, working remotely across multiple countries and time zones. We design new pitches, projects and plans within our group, and get it done.
