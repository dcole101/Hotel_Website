About the Film

Dead Tired is a three-minute animated short film about an exhausted businessman who checks into a rundown hotel, only to encounter a ghost with a talent for mischief and a desperate need for attention.

The film follows an unexpected encounter between two characters. What begins as an attempt to frighten an unsuspecting guest soon becomes a story about overworking, and finding friendship in the most unexpected places.

Meet the Characters

Greg

The Exhausted Businessman

Greg is an overworked businessman whose life revolves around paperwork and his work phone. So consumed by his responsibilities is he that he barely notices the world around him, making him the perfect target for a mischievous ghost.

Despite the strange happenings around him, Greg has only one thing on his mind: getting some much-needed sleep.

The Bellhop

A Prankster Ghost

Once a hotel bellhop in the 1940s, Bellhop has spent decades haunting the Deepwoods Hotel. Although he desperately wants to frighten its visitors, his attempts at being terrifying tend to be more silly than sinister.

He can levitate objects with his ghostly powers, but his ability to be seen by the living comes with a peculiar condition. Humans can only see his ghostly form through mirrors unless he chooses to reveal himself.

The Deepwoods Hotel

Inspired by the Crescent Hotel, the Deepwoods Hotel is an aging establishment with an atmosphere reminiscent of the 1980s.

The hotel has been haunted for so long that few guests dare to stay, leaving its halls quiet and its rooms largely forgotten.

About Electric Spuds

Electric Spuds is a collaborative team of Sheridan College Computer Animation alumni who came together to create an original animated short film.

With 14 artists bringing their individual skills and creative perspectives to the project, we set out to develop a film that would allow us to continue growing as artists while creating new portfolio pieces.

Beginning with a collection of ideas and a shared enthusiasm for storytelling, we developed Dead Tired from the ground up. Our goal is to create an entertaining short film that showcases our collective skills in visual storytelling.

For us, Dead Tired is an opportunity to collaborate beyond the classroom, challenge ourselves creatively, and bring an original story to life.

Our Production Journey

Development on Dead Tired began in April 2026, when our team started pitching ideas and exploring possible directions for the film. After selecting our concept, we moved into scripting and developing the characters, then moving to storyboarding, props, and environments that would define its visual identity.

With the initial story and designs established, we progressed into modelling, rigging, and texturing while beginning the layout process.
